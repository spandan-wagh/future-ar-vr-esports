# Empirical Research

## Effect of Interaction Latency on Competitive Performance in Immersive Environments

This directory contains the empirical research phase of the **Future AR/VR Esports** project.

The first phase of the project consisted of a 19-day exploratory study of technologies underlying modern AR and VR systems, including tracking, motion-to-photon latency, OpenXR, SLAM, hand and eye tracking, mixed reality, spatial interaction, stereo rendering, and asynchronous reprojection.

That exploration identified **latency and interaction responsiveness** as recurring technical factors with potential implications for competitive immersive experiences. This research phase develops that observation into a focused empirical investigation.

## Research Direction

The study investigates how controlled interaction latency affects human performance during time-sensitive target-acquisition tasks in an immersive environment.

The initial research question is:

> **How does controlled interaction latency affect target-acquisition performance in a competitive immersive task?**

The study will examine measurable performance outcomes such as:

* Target-acquisition time
* Accuracy and error rate
* Reaction time
* Performance changes across latency conditions

The final research question, hypotheses, latency conditions, experimental procedure, and statistical analysis plan will be established after reviewing the relevant academic literature.

## Research Process

The project will progress through the following stages:

1. **Literature Review**
   Review existing research on XR latency, human performance, target acquisition, pointing and selection, immersive interaction, and competitive gaming latency.

2. **Research Gap Identification**
   Determine what is already established and identify a specific question that can be meaningfully investigated.

3. **Research Design**
   Define the research question, hypotheses, variables, controls, participant procedure, performance metrics, exclusion criteria, and analysis methodology.

4. **External Methodology Review**
   Seek feedback from researchers working in relevant areas such as HCI, XR, VR, and interactive systems before final data collection.

5. **Preregistration**
   Publicly document the hypotheses and analysis plan before examining the final experimental results.

6. **Experimental System**
   Develop a controlled immersive experimental environment and automated data-collection system.

7. **Data Collection**
   Conduct the experiment under the finalized research protocol and applicable ethical requirements.

8. **Analysis**
   Analyze the collected data using the statistical methods specified by the research design and report effect sizes, uncertainty, and relevant limitations.

9. **Manuscript and Reproducibility**
   Prepare a research manuscript and make appropriate experimental materials, analysis code, and non-identifying research artifacts publicly available.

## Current Status

**Stage:** Literature Review & Research Gap Identification

The proposed research question and experimental design are preliminary. They may change as the literature review establishes what previous research has already demonstrated and where a meaningful research gap exists.

## Research Principles

This project aims to maintain:

* Clear separation between exploratory learning and empirical research
* Transparent documentation of methodological decisions
* Reproducible experimental procedures
* Hypotheses established before final data analysis
* Honest reporting of negative, inconclusive, and positive results
* Clear acknowledgement of limitations
* Appropriate treatment of human-participant data and research ethics
